# Mapflyover FAQ

Short answers about Mapflyover ([mapflyover.com](https://mapflyover.com)). For anything not covered here, email [support@mapflyover.com](mailto:support@mapflyover.com).

## The product

**What is Mapflyover?**
A web app that turns any place on Earth into a cinematic 3D map flyover video. You arrange places into scenes, add a script, and Mapflyover generates AI narration and word-timed captions and flies a cinematic camera between the places. Videos export as MP4 for YouTube, Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

**What does "3D" mean here?**
A cinematic camera effect: the camera tilts to an angled view (up to 85°) over real map imagery with terrain relief, with pans, zooms and orbits between scenes. It is not modelled 3D buildings.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. Mapflyover runs in a modern web browser, and exports render in your browser too.

**Do I need video-editing experience?**
No. The editor is scene-based and built for non-editors: pick places, write a script, export.

**Do I need my own API keys?**
No. Narration is free and captions are timed from the narration, so everything works without a key. A free Mapbox public token is only needed if you export the satellite look, and an optional Groq key only if you want captions for a recording you made yourself.

## Plans and exports

**What can I do for free?**
Everything in the studio: build scenes, boundaries and stat and focus cards, import scene JSON, preview as often as you like, and create narration and captions. The Free plan also exports 2 videos a month at 720p (up to 90 seconds each) with the Mapflyover mark. Free does not expire.

**What video formats can I export?**
MP4 in vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9. There is no square (1:1) export. Resolution is 720p on Free, up to 1080p on Pro and up to 4K on Studio; maximum length is 90 seconds on Free, 5 minutes on Pro and 15 minutes on Studio.

**What do the plan limits count?**
Exports reset every billing month, on the day your subscription renews. Projects are a running total (3 Free / 50 Pro / 500 Studio). A failed export does not count against you. Narration and captions are not limited by your plan.

**Can I try Pro first?**
Yes — 7 days for USD $3 (8 exports at 720p, with the Mapflyover mark). On day 8 it becomes Pro at USD $20 a month unless you cancel first; cancel before day 8 and the $20 is never charged. One trial per account.

**How do I pay, and can I cancel?**
By card, through Stripe; Mapflyover never sees your card details. You can cancel, change plan, update your card or download invoices from the billing page at any time. After cancelling you keep your plan until the end of the paid period, then return to Free with your projects kept. Paying locally from Pakistan via WhatsApp is also possible, at a discount.

**Do you offer refunds?**
See the [refund policy](https://mapflyover.com/refund) for full details.

## AI agents

**Can my AI agent make videos in Mapflyover?**
Yes. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client at [mapflyover.com/agents](https://mapflyover.com/agents) with a single OAuth 2.0 sign-in. The agent builds the project — scenes, narration, captions — from your description.

**What can't an agent do?**
Agents cannot export videos and cannot spend credits or change billing. You review the project and export it yourself in the editor. See [agents-and-mcp.md](agents-and-mcp.md).

**Is agent connection a paid feature?**
No. It is available on every plan, including Free.

## Accounts and support

**How do I sign up?**
At [mapflyover.com](https://mapflyover.com), with a Google account or email. No credit card is needed for the Free plan.

**How do I get help?**
Signed-in users can open a support ticket inside the app; ticket updates and replies are also sent by email. Anyone can email [support@mapflyover.com](mailto:support@mapflyover.com) or use the [contact page](https://mapflyover.com/contact). Pro and Studio include priority support; Free includes community support.
