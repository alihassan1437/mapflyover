# Mapflyover pricing

**Canonical source:** [mapflyover.com/pricing](https://mapflyover.com/pricing) — if anything here ever differs, the website is correct.

Building is free on every plan: the full editor, unlimited previews, AI narration and captions cost nothing. Plans decide how much video you can export, at what resolution, and whether exports carry the Mapflyover mark. All prices are in US dollars.

Every plan also includes:

- The full editor: scenes, cards, boundaries, JSON import
- Unlimited previews
- Natural narration and timed captions, free, no keys needed
- Connect your own AI agent (MCP)
- MP4 export in 9:16 or 16:9, rendered in your browser

## The Free plan

Free is a real plan, not a trial: it does not expire, and your projects stay in your account. It exports 2 MP4 videos a month at 720p, up to 90 seconds each, with the Mapflyover mark. You only pay when you want more, longer or sharper exports without the mark.

## The Pro trial

Try Pro for 7 days for USD $3: 8 exports at 720p, with the Mapflyover mark. On day 8 the trial becomes Pro at USD $20 a month — no mark, 1080p, 30 exports a month — unless you cancel first. Cancel before day 8 and the USD $20 is never charged. One trial per account.

## Pro vs Studio

Studio raises the monthly export count (300 vs 30), allows 4K instead of 1080p, longer videos (15 min vs 5 min) and more projects (500 vs 50). API access and team seats are not currently offered on any plan.

## Billing notes

- Subscriptions are billed by card through Stripe and renew monthly until cancelled; you can cancel any time and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, after which the account returns to Free and your projects are kept.
- Export allowances reset every billing month, on the day your subscription renews. Project counts are a running total. A failed export does not count against you. Narration and captions are not limited by your plan.
- No credit card is needed to start on Free.
- Paying locally from Pakistan is possible through WhatsApp, at a discount (20% off for 1 month, 30% off for 3 months); once the payment is confirmed, the plan is switched on.
- Refunds: see the [refund policy](https://mapflyover.com/refund).
