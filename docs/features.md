# Mapflyover features

Mapflyover is a browser-based studio for making cinematic map flyover videos. Everything below is available at [mapflyover.com](https://mapflyover.com); building and previewing are free on every plan.

## The editor

- **Scene-based editing** — a video is a sequence of scenes, each centred on a real place. Search for a place, add it as a scene, and order the scenes into a route or a story.
- **Stat and focus cards** — overlay cards that present facts, figures and highlights about a place at the right moment in the video.
- **Real place boundaries** — outline countries, states, cities and other real boundaries on the map as part of a scene.
- **Scene JSON import** — import scene data as JSON to build or reproduce projects programmatically.
- **Unlimited previews** — preview the full video as often as you like while you edit. Only exports count against your plan.
- **Projects** — save multiple projects in your account (3 on Free, 50 on Pro, 500 on Studio).

## The map and the camera

- **10 map styles**, including a satellite look.
- **Cinematic camera moves** — the camera flies between scenes with pans, zooms and orbit moves.
- **Angled "3D" view** — the camera tilts up to 85° over real map imagery with terrain relief, giving the flyover its 3D feel. This is a camera and terrain effect, not modelled 3D buildings.

## Narration

- **48 AI voices** to narrate your script (scripts can be up to 20,000 characters).
- Narration is generated by Mapflyover and is **free on every plan** — no API key is needed.
- Optional: add your own Groq key in Settings only if you want captions generated for a recording you made yourself.

## Captions

- **Word-timed captions** — captions are generated from the narration, so they stay in sync with the voice.
- **7 caption presets** and **8 fonts**, including **word-highlighted caption styles** that highlight each word as it is spoken.
- Captions are not limited by your plan.

## Export

- **Format:** MP4, in vertical **9:16** or landscape **16:9**. There is no square (1:1) export.
- **Resolution:** 720p on Free, up to 1080p on Pro, up to 4K on Studio.
- **Length:** up to 90 seconds per export on Free, 5 minutes on Pro, 15 minutes on Studio.
- **Rendered in your browser** — the export runs on your own machine.
- **The Mapflyover mark:** Free and Pro-trial exports carry the Mapflyover mark; Pro and Studio exports have no watermark.
- A failed export does not count against your monthly allowance.

## AI agent connection (MCP)

- Connect **Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client** with a single OAuth 2.0 sign-in.
- Your agent can create and build projects — scenes, narration and captions — from a plain-language description.
- Agents **cannot export videos or spend credits**; you review and export in the editor yourself.
- Available on every plan, including Free. See [agents-and-mcp.md](agents-and-mcp.md).

## Sharing

- **Share to Buffer** on Pro and Studio: send finished videos to your social channels using your own Buffer account token.

## Platform facts

- Runs entirely in a modern web browser — nothing to install.
- Sign-in is with a Google account or email.
- Support is built in: signed-in users get ticketed support in the app, and anyone can email [support@mapflyover.com](mailto:support@mapflyover.com).
