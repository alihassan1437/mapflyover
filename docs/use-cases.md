# Mapflyover use cases

Mapflyover ([mapflyover.com](https://mapflyover.com)) makes one kind of video very well: a cinematic flyover of real places, narrated and captioned, ready for social platforms. These are the jobs people most often use it for.

## Travel creators

Turn an itinerary into a video: fly from stop to stop along a road trip, a rail journey or an island-hopping route, with narration telling the story of each place. Vertical 9:16 exports suit Shorts, Reels and TikTok; 16:9 suits YouTube. Countdown and ranking formats — the ten tallest mountains, the longest rivers, state-by-state guides — are a natural fit for the scene-based editor.

## Geography and history education

Show, don't just tell. A lesson on a river system, a historical trade route such as the Silk Road, a partition or a migration becomes a journey across the actual map, with boundaries outlined and facts presented in stat cards as the camera arrives at each place. Teachers can build a video once and reuse it every term; students can make their own as a project.

## Real estate

Location is the product. A flyover that starts wide — the city, the suburb, the coast — and settles onto a property's neighbourhood gives buyers and renters the context that still photos can't: distance to the beach, the station, the schools, the centre. Agents can produce a consistent, narrated location video for every listing without a video editor on staff.

## Local business and tourism

Hotels, tour operators, attractions and local services use flyovers to show exactly where they are and what surrounds them: the route from the airport, nearby landmarks, the shape of a tour. It answers the first question every customer has — "where is it?" — in the first three seconds of a video.

## Social and short-form content

Map videos perform as short-form content because the movement holds attention. Mapflyover's vertical exports, word-highlighted captions and narration are designed for that format: a single striking fact about a place, counted down or compared, becomes a finished Short or Reel without editing software.

## Data and ranking stories

Any list with places in it is a video waiting to be made: biggest, smallest, oldest, richest, wettest, most remote. Put the entries in as scenes, let the narration count them down, and export — by hand in the editor, or by describing the video to a connected AI agent and letting it build the project ([agents-and-mcp.md](agents-and-mcp.md)).
